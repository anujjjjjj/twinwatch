Twinwatch is a tool we're building to find literal clones of your indie app or game: copies that reuse your name, icon, screenshots, assets or code. It puts together dated proof and gives you a notice in the format each platform asks for. You review it and file it yourself.

We're building this now. Early sign-ups get a free scan of their product, which we run by hand.

Where it works, and where it doesn't

Takedowns work when the copy is literal: same or confusingly similar name, your icon, your screenshots or trailer, your art and audio files, your code. That is where Twinwatch focuses. If someone rebuilt your idea from scratch, that is usually not copyright infringement, and we'll tell you so plainly instead of selling you a notice.

What we're building

Right now early-access scans are run by hand, and the automated pieces below are in progress.

  • Clone radar: App Store, Google Play, Steam, itch.io, GitHub (including fork networks) and npm, matched by name, icon, screenshots, store copy and code strings.
  • Evidence pack: timestamped screenshots, archived copies of listings, side-by-side comparisons, plus your proof of being first.
  • Notices ready to file: App Store content dispute, Google Play IP complaint, Steam IP complaint, GitHub DMCA under §512(c) with the per-repo list it requires, and host abuse reports.
  • Tracker: what was filed, when, and any counter-notice deadlines.
